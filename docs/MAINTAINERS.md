## How to reach us

* **Security vulnerabilities:** See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) — email the project lead; do not open public issues.
* **Bugs and features:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/YOUR_ORG/wia-core/issues) (update org/repo URL when published).
* **Contributing:** [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Response expectations

* Security reports: acknowledgment within a few business days.
* Issues and PRs: best-effort review; campus term schedules may affect turnaround.

## Adding maintainers

New maintainers are added by existing maintainers after sustained contributions and agreement on the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
