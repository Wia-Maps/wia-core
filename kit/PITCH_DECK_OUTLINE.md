# WIA Executive Pitch (Slide Outline)

**Slide 1: Title**
* WIA: The Smart Campus Operating System
* Subtitle: Indigenous Digital Infrastructure for [Insert University Name]

**Slide 2: The Problem (The "Blind" Campus)**
* Reliance on generic, outdated third-party maps.
* Inaccurate pedestrian routing for students and visitors.
* Zero integration with campus utilities or emergency response protocols.

**Slide 3: The Solution (The Digital Twin)**
* WIA provides a proprietary, locally-hosted digital twin of the campus.
* **Sub-meter accuracy:** Maps actual footpaths, not just roads.
* **Dynamic Data:** Capable of showing real-time facility status.

**Slide 4: The Institutional Advantage**
* **Rankings & Prestige:** Demonstrates high-level R&D and technological output (Webometrics/NUC).
* **Data Sovereignty:** The institution completely controls its spatial data and security nodes.
* **Safety:** Direct, pinpoint routing for emergency and medical dispatch.

**Slide 5: The "Producer" Narrative**
* We are no longer just consuming technology; our students are building it. 
* Adopting WIA signals to stakeholders that [University Name] is a Grade-A tech-forward institution.

**Slide 6: The Ask**
* Official endorsement to run the beta pilot.
* Administrative support to deploy QR-markers across key campus zones.
